# Third-party assets and reference material

## Distributed surface textures

The project includes the following Poly Haven scanned surface textures at 1K resolution:

- [Concrete Layers 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_layers_02)
- [Brown Mud Dry](https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud_dry)

Poly Haven makes these assets available under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). See [Poly Haven's asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license). Diffuse, roughness, DirectX normal and OpenGL normal maps are recorded individually with source URLs and checksums in [provenance.json](SourceAssets/Textures/provenance.json). The Unreal texture assets derive from the same maps.

## Research references

UC San Diego, Stuart Collection, the original Pereira report, ArchDaily and the credited photographers supplied the architectural and landscape references. Their photographs and drawings are **not distributed in this repository**. The local research copies remain outside version control. [References/README.md](References/README.md) records source links, credits and known license information, including a separately attributed CC BY-SA photograph.

The reconstructed geometry, procedural materials, vegetation, scene scripts and screenshots were created for this project. The scene depicts Geisel Library and Alexis Smith's Snake Path; the rights in the depicted architecture and artwork remain with their respective rights holders. Public repository access does not assign a blanket license to the project. No license for the project-created files has been selected.

## Software requirements

Blender and Unreal Engine are required tools; their executables are not included. Obtain them from their official distributions and follow their respective software terms. This is an independent reconstruction and is not affiliated with or endorsed by UC San Diego.
